## What is already settled on our side

- AlgeriaPos is the company's official accounting system; all the books are kept in it.
- Our own team reviews the chart of accounts against the SCF.
- Equipment hire carries VAT, and the rent for the agreed period is booked on the first day of the rental.
- VAT is optional when the bill is made: whoever makes it chooses taxed or untaxed, on till sales, invoices and rental bills (owner, 2026-11-02).
- Rental equipment is a fixed asset, not merchandise stock.
- Months are closed after review; nothing can then be posted into a closed month, and mistakes are fixed through a controlled, recorded correction.
- Payslips and deductions stay as they are today.
- Money received for a gift card is a debt to the holder until the card is used.
- A sale is never blocked by a missing product cost; such sales are flagged and their margin shown as incomplete.
